Meet The Founder

The Story of Smart Advertising & Anand Awad

Smart Advertising Flex Printing was founded by Anand Awad with a clear vision: to provide local retail businesses, real estate developers, event organizers, and contractors in Punawale and PCMC with reliable, high-definition outdoor printing and dependable turnaround.

Located conveniently on the Mumbai-Bangalore Highway Service Road near the Punawale Underpass, our modern print shop houses industrial digital solvent printers and heavy-duty laminators. Under Anand Awad's personal supervision, every banner is quality-checked for true color saturation, precise dimension accuracy, and robust finishing before handover.

Our Printing Machinery & Capabilities

We operate industrial-grade digital printing equipment on-site so you receive vibrant color reproduction with zero delay.

Large-Format Solvent Printers

High-speed 10.5 ft wide solvent roll-to-roll printers for billboards, hoardings, and outdoor frontlit flex banners capable of continuous high-volume printing.

Eco-Solvent Vinyl Printing

Photographic 1440 DPI micro-piezo heads providing sharp detail, smooth skin tones, and rich graphics for indoor displays, vehicle wraps, and menu boards.

Sunboard & Acrylic Mounting

Heavy-duty motorized cold/hot laminators and precision substrate cutting tables to guarantee 100% bubble-free application onto Sunboard, Sunpack, and Acrylic.

Fabrication & Sign Fitting

Welding, metal box framing, LED wiring, and on-site ladder/scaffolding installation by trained signboard technicians across Pune.
